Your rules, enforced

You decide what she may agree to

Set it once in the owner's desk. She works inside it, and anything more comes to you.

She may

  • Accept a date for the whole payment, up to 7 days away
  • Accept part payments, if all of it arrives within 15 days
  • Note RTGS, cheque, UPI or cash
  • Promise the statement or bank details on WhatsApp
  • Send rate and stock questions to the person they deal with

She never

  • Offers a discount, or waives interest
  • Agrees to a date past your limit
  • Says an amount that is on no bill
  • Threatens, or mentions legal action
  • Tells anyone else what is owed

How it works

Four steps, and the first one you already do

  1. 01

    Dues come from your ledger

    Every bill and payment, with due dates. Speak entries by voice in Gujarati, or start from your outstanding list.

  2. 02

    She calls who's due

    In your call hours: overdue bills, missed promises, and repeat calls every few days.

  3. 03

    She gets a commitment

    Date, amount and mode, within your limits. Disputes, discounts and long dates are handed to you.

  4. 04

    You read the sheet

    Outcome, promise, your to-do list and the full conversation, seconds after each call.
