    Your rules, enforced

    You decide what she may agree to

    Set it once in the owner's desk. She works inside it, and anything more comes to you.

    She may

    • Accept a date for the whole payment, up to 7 days away
    • Accept parts, if all of it arrives within 15 days
    • Note RTGS, cheque, UPI or cash
    • Promise the statement on WhatsApp
    • Send rate and stock questions to your staff

    She never

    • Offers a discount or waives interest
    • Agrees to a date past your limit
    • Says an amount that's on no bill
    • Threatens or mentions legal action
    • Tells anyone else what is owed

    How it works

    Four steps, and you already do the first

    1. 01

      Dues from your ledger

      Every bill and payment with its due date, spoken in Gujarati or taken from your outstanding list.

    2. 02

      She calls who's due

      In your call hours: overdue bills, missed promises and repeat calls every few days.

    3. 03

      She gets a commitment

      A date, an amount and a mode, within your limits. She hands disputes and discounts to you.

    4. 04

      You read the sheet

      The outcome, the promise, your to-do list and the conversation, seconds after each call.
